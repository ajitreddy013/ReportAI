AUTOMATIC ROOFTOP CURTAINS USING TEMPERATURE SENSOR & ARDUINO UNO
1.1 INTRODUCTION

In this era of fast-paced life, smart solutions and automation are becoming an essential part of open-air cafes with motorized rooftop curtains. The system uses a DS18B20 temperature sensor and Arduino Uno to open and close rooftop curtains automatically based on ambient heat.

Manual curtain operation requires continuous staff labor and delays response during sudden weather changes. The proposed system deploys an L293D motor driver connected to a 12V DC gear motor to actuate the canvas shade.

2.1 LITERATURE SURVEY

A survey of motorized cafe shade mechanisms and microcontroller threshold switching was conducted across published works on Arduino-based electromechanical actuators and temperature sensors.

3.1 PROPOSED METHODOLOGY & IMPLEMENTATION

The hardware setup connects the DS18B20 sensor data pin to digital pin 2 of the Arduino Uno. Motor driver inputs are wired to pins 4 and 5 on a breadboard. When temperature exceeds 35°C, the motor turns clockwise to extend the curtain.

4.2 RESULT AND DISCUSSION

Experimental trials on a cafe model verified that the curtain fully deployed within 8 seconds when hot air was applied to the temperature sensor, proving feasibility for outdoor dining comfort.

5.1 CONCLUSION

The automatic rooftop curtain prototype successfully demonstrates temperature-triggered motorized actuation for cafe shading, satisfying university evaluation requirements.
